About Our School

Learning Beyond TEXTBOOKS

Established in 2012, Maragathaam Matriculation Higher Secondary School serves learners in Perambalur with English-medium education from Kindergarten to Grade XII. Guided by its stated commitment to holistic learning and intellectual development, the school seeks to create an educational experience that values academic progress alongside personal growth. The school’s educational direction is rooted in preparing students for lifelong success. Its learning environment is supported by classrooms, laboratories, a library, digital learning resources and opportunities for physical development. Through a balanced emphasis on learning, values and student welfare, Maragathaam aims to help young people approach their next stage of education with confidence.
