OUR LEARNING APPROACH

Growing Through Every Experience.

Meaningful experiences that nurture curiosity, encourage participation and support the development of young learners.

Curriculum

The curriculum focuses on foundational literacy, numeracy, communication and age-appropriate awareness of the world.

Teaching Methodology

Storytelling, conversation, play-based exploration, songs and guided hands-on tasks make learning enjoyable.

Learning Activities

Picture talk, creative work, group games and movement activities encourage active participation.

Skill Development

Language confidence, listening, coordination, cooperation and independence are encouraged.

Learning Outcomes

Children begin to express ideas, recognise simple patterns and participate confidently in everyday routines.

Holistic Growth

Children are encouraged to build positive relationships, express themselves and develop confidence through experiences.
